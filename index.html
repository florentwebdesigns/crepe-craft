02 The craft

How a crepe comes together.

  1. i

    Spread & drizzle

    A thin pour on a hot griddle, spread wide until the edges turn golden and lacy. Then the chocolate goes on.

  2. ii

    Fold

    Half, then half again. The classic triangle that keeps everything inside.

  3. iii

    Top it off

    Fruit, another drizzle and a snowfall of sugar. Sweet or savory, your call.
