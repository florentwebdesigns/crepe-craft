## Files

- `index.html`: generated by `src-3d/build.mjs` from `src-3d/index.src.html` (the menu tabs and the static menu list come from `menu.js`). Edit the template, then rebuild. All text is real HTML; canvases are `aria-hidden`.
- `assets/style.css`: tokens and styles (see `DESIGN.md`). Hand-edited.
- `assets/app.js`: party form (blur-first validation, builds a mailto with subject and body, success panel) and the loader for the 3D layer (`import('./scene.js')` after load; any failure switches to the no-WebGL page).
- `assets/scene.js`: the prebuilt three.js 0.187.0 bundle (about 675 KB minified, about 180 KB gzipped). Do not edit by hand.
- `assets/media/stills/`: no-WebGL fallback stills rendered from the live scene (`hero.webp` 1600×1000, `sweet/savory/waffles/drinks.webp` 800×600). Re-render with `src-3d/stills.js` after a look change.
- `assets/media/sticker.webp`: the cafe's logo sticker on the drink cups. `assets/media/og-1200.jpg`: social preview (the cafe's strawberry waffle photo).
- `src-3d/`: source of the 3D layer (excluded from deploys by `.vercelignore`).
  - `src/menu.js` menu data + category moods; `food.js` builds crepes, waffles, drinks and toppings; `geo.js` geometry (crepe folds, waffle grid, drizzle tubes); `tex.js` procedural canvas textures (cached); `mats.js` materials (powdered-sugar `dusted()` shader per material); `stage.js` renderer, studio light, pedestal, post effects; `intro.js` the intro timeline; `menu3d.js` the carousel; `main.js` render loop and page UI; `anim.js` easing helpers.
  - Build: `cd src-3d && npm i && node build.mjs` writes `../index.html` and `../assets/scene.js`.
  - Screenshots: `node pshot.js <outdir> <w> <h> '<steps json>' [query]` (steps `{name, sec:'intro'|'menu'|selector, p}`, `{name, index}`, `{name, y}`); `node stills.js [hero|menu]` renders the fallback stills. Both need Playwright and a `fontcache/` (fonts.css + woff2). Run one Playwright job at a time: two SwiftShader contexts in one process ran out of memory.
- Test URLs: `?shot` (no easing, for screenshots), `?still` (force the no-WebGL page).
- `_archive-v2/`, `_archive-v3/`, `_archive-v4/`: retired versions (v4 = the owner-photo version with GSAP/Lenis). Excluded from deploys. Safe to delete.
- `DESIGN.md`: design system. Follow it for any change.

## Design decisions (v5, Nov 2026)

- florent's brief: a cinematic 3D product experience in the style of ciaoenergy.com. Live three.js food on a dark moody stage, **no photos, videos or GIFs for the items**, big object, short name in large type, scroll-scrubbed and reversible.
- Everything is live three.js; **no 2.5D items**. Food is procedural (no downloaded models).
- Powdered sugar is shown only where the board lists it (Crepe Craft). Toppings follow each item's board ingredients.
- Intro (640vh, sticky stage): hero crepe in front of a faint "Crepe Craft" wordmark → spin-in → batter poured and spread on a griddle → edges turn golden → two folds → Nutella drizzle, strawberries, sugar → texture close-up → exploded view with labels → snaps back → the crepe sweeps across and wipes in the logo → "Crepes & Waffles", address, Order online → slides into the menu. Skip intro link.
- Menu: one sticky stage, scroll picks the item (Sweet → Savory → Waffles → Drinks), arrows/keys/swipe/tabs scroll for you, drag spins with inertia, each newcomer spins in with a bounce. Category moods change the backdrop and rim light.
- One WebGL canvas per stage, each paused off-screen; menu items build lazily, the rest warm up when idle. DPR cap 2 (1.75 on phones).
- Reduced motion keeps the scroll story, drops float, idle spin, steam and particles. No WebGL → static page with the rendered stills and the full menu list.
- Phone + Order online are always visible: fixed top bar, and a fixed bottom action bar on phones.
