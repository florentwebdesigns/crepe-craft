## Files

- `index.html`: generated by `src-3d/build.mjs` from `src-3d/index.src.html` (the menu tabs and the static menu list come from `menu.js`). Edit the template, then rebuild. All text is real HTML; canvases are `aria-hidden`.
- `assets/style.css`: tokens and styles (see `DESIGN.md`). Hand-edited.
- `assets/app.js`: booking request form (table / party / group order cards, date, time, guests stepper, name, phone, optional email and notes; blur-first validation; builds a mailto with subject and body; success panel) and the loader for the 3D layer (`import('./scene.js')` after load; any failure switches to the no-WebGL page; the hero words show after 6 s even if 3D is slow).
- `assets/scene.js`: the prebuilt three.js 0.187.0 bundle (about 685 KB minified, about 185 KB gzipped). Do not edit by hand.
- `assets/media/stills/`: no-WebGL fallback stills rendered from the live scene (`hero.webp` 1600×1000 and `hero-p.webp` 780×1300 for phones, both the final hero frame; `sweet/savory/waffles/drinks.webp` 800×600). Re-render with `src-3d/stills.js` after a look change.
- `assets/media/sticker.webp`: the cafe's logo sticker on the drink cups. `assets/media/og-1200.jpg`: social preview (the cafe's strawberry waffle photo).
- `src-3d/`: source of the 3D layer (excluded from deploys by `.vercelignore`).
  - `src/menu.js` menu data + category moods; `food.js` builds crepes, waffles, drinks and toppings; `geo.js` geometry (crepe folds, waffle grid, drizzle tubes; `surfaceY` uses a cached height grid, not raycasts); `tex.js` procedural canvas textures (cached); `mats.js` materials (powdered-sugar `dusted()` shader per material); `stage.js` renderer, studio light, pedestal, post effects, adaptive resolution; `backdrop.js` the moving molten caramel/chocolate background (palettes per section); `hero.js` the 4.6 s automatic hero shot; `logo3d.js` + `logo-data.js` the 3D logo; `menu3d.js` the carousel; `main.js` render loop and page UI; `anim.js` easing helpers.
  - The 3D logo is rebuilt from the cafe's sticker with Google Fonts Leckerli One ("Crepe") and Titan One ("CRAFT"): `python3 logo_build.py` (needs uharfbuzz, fontTools, pyclipper and the two .ttf files) writes `src/logo-data.js`.
  - `intro.js` is the retired v5 scroll intro, kept for reference and not bundled.
  - Build: `cd src-3d && npm i && node build.mjs` writes `../index.html` and `../assets/scene.js`.
  - Screenshots: `node pshot.js <outdir> <w> <h> '<steps json>' [query]` (steps `{name, sec:'hero'|'menu'|selector, p, hero: seconds}`, `{name, index}`, `{name, y}`); `node stills.js [hero|menu]` renders the fallback stills. Both need Playwright and a `fontcache/` (fonts.css + woff2). Run one Playwright job at a time: two SwiftShader contexts in one process ran out of memory.
- Test URLs: `?shot` (fixed resolution, no easing, for screenshots), `?still` (force the no-WebGL page). `window.__cc.hero.clock = 3` jumps the hero shot to 3 s.
- `_archive-v2/` to `_archive-v5/`: retired versions (v4 = the owner-photo version with GSAP/Lenis, v5 = the 640vh scroll intro). Excluded from deploys. Safe to delete.
- `DESIGN.md`: design system. Follow it for any change.

## Design decisions (v6, Nov 2026)

- florent's v6 brief: lead-focused site (Order now goes to Seamless everywhere, plus a big booking form); a short, automatic, cinematic 3D intro instead of the long scroll intro; no lag; a creative moving background tied to the business.
- Everything is live three.js; **no photos, videos or GIFs for the items, no 2.5D items**. Food is procedural (no downloaded models).
- **Hero (100svh, automatic, 4.6 s, no scrolling):** a crepe falls onto a dark stoneware plate and settles → a Nutella stream draws the drizzle → strawberries and banana fall on one by one → powdered sugar snows down → the 3D Crepe Craft logo drops in behind with a heavy bounce while the camera eases back and the plate glides forward → the words and buttons rise in (Order now, Book a table, address, hours). Any key, tap, wheel or scroll jumps to the end; Replay intro button. The hero recipe follows florent's brief (Nutella, fruit, sugar), not one board item.
- **Background (sleek, per florent 2026-11-20):** near-black espresso with a warm glow behind the subject and faint golden batter-spiral rings turning slowly out from it, like a crepe spread on the griddle (`backdrop.js` style 1; an earlier busy molten-caramel look was rejected as too much). Drawn at about a quarter of the screen size, at most 30 times a second. Palettes: hero caramel; menu Sweet chocolate, Savory butter, Waffles maple, Drinks berry.
- Menu: one sticky stage, scroll picks the item (Sweet → Savory → Waffles → Drinks), arrows/keys/swipe/tabs scroll for you, drag spins with inertia, each newcomer spins in with a bounce.
- **Speed:** one WebGL canvas per stage, each paused off-screen and when the tab is hidden; resolution starts at 1.5× and adapts to the device every second (drops when frames run slow, then turns bloom off; climbs back when there is headroom); shadow map 1024 (768 phones); the hero compiles every shader before its clock starts; the menu is built only after the hero shot and its items warm up during idle time when the visitor is not scrolling.
- **Booking:** a mailto request form (no backend). Type cards (table, party, group order), date, time slots within posted hours, guests stepper, name, phone, optional email and notes. Copy says nothing is booked until the cafe confirms. Side panel: Order now, phone, hours, address.
- Reduced motion: the hero shows its finished frame at once; menu keeps the scroll story without float, idle spin, steam or particles. No WebGL → static page with the rendered stills and the full menu list.
- Phone, Book and Order now are always visible: fixed top bar, and a fixed Call / Book / Order now bar on phones.
